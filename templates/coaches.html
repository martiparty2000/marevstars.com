Football biography {% if coach.image == 'todor.png' %}

Todor Boychev Marev is a legendary Bulgarian footballer and defender. He holds Cherno More Varna’s A Group appearance record with 422 matches. Born in 1953, he came through every level of the club and made his first-team debut in 1972. He played 530 official matches for Cherno More in total — a national record for appearances with one club. For Bulgaria, he made 16 appearances for the senior national team and 36 for youth teams. He won the Footballer of Varna award in three consecutive seasons: 1976, 1977 and 1978. After finishing his playing career, he coached in Cherno More’s academy and led Grand Hotel Varna’s women’s team to two league titles.

{% elif coach.image == 'marev.png' %}

Blagovest Todorov Marev was born on 21 April 1983 in Varna, Bulgaria. A Cherno More academy graduate, he later played for Dobrudzha and Kaliakra. From 2007 to 2009 he was part of MFC Varna, one of Bulgaria’s leading futsal teams. In 2009–2010 he played for Croatia’s Bitumina, then returned to Bulgaria and represented Grand Pro, MFC Varna and Odessos Hidroremont. In 2013 he joined Malta’s Hibernians and became a Maltese champion. His time there was also linked with some of the Bulgarian national futsal team’s strongest results.
